Lesson 11 – Get ready for…

Grade 7 – Unit 2: Animals – Exam practice (A2 Key for Schools)

You are a junior reporter for Wild Kids magazine! Read, listen, speak and write to pass the Reporter Challenge. Choose any channel to start – you can play the stages in any order!

👩‍🏫 Teacher's lesson plan (25 minutes)

Lesson aims: to practise for the A2 Key for Schools Reading and Writing Parts 3 and 5, Speaking Part 1 (Phase 2), A2 Flyers Speaking Part 4 and A2 Key for Schools Listening Part 3.
Target language: unit vocabulary and grammar.
Teacher's Book: Lesson 11, pages 128–130 – Class Book and Activity Book “Get ready for…”. Materials: Unit 2 poster, flashcards, Traffic light cards.
Note: the texts and the listening script in this game are new practice texts written in the style of the exam tasks. The Class Book and Activity Book tasks themselves are done in the books.
